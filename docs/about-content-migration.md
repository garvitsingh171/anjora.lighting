## Section mapping

- About hero: legacy “Transforming Ambience, One Beam at a Time” and “Crafting Light to Elevate Environments” positioning.
- Our company: company location, independent-consultancy positioning, team, project spectrum, global practices, sustainability and Indian-market context from the About page.
- Philosophy: the published idea of light as a controllable spatial-design tool alongside form, scale and material.
- Founder: Shubham Khandelwal’s published biography and direct contact from the About and Founder pages.
- Education: Electronics and Communication Engineering, M.Tech in VLSI at IIT Roorkee, and the source’s stated Illumination Technology study at Oakland University in the Netherlands.
- Career: the source’s 2018 Lighting Designer / Jaquar + Philips Lighting and 2024 Director / Anjora Lighting milestones.
- Principles and closing: published themes of artistry, innovation, functionality, emotion, contemporary elegance and “Lighting isn’t just illumination—it’s experience.”

## Source cautions

- The legacy page repeats the M.Tech in VLSI credential. The redesigned page includes it once.
- The source describes Oakland University as being in the Netherlands. This wording has been retained conservatively and not independently “corrected.”
- No founding year, project count, client count, awards, team size or other unsupported metrics were added.
